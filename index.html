Lab lessons

Each lesson goes with one lab. Read it before you open the lab in Q Circuits: it explains the parts in the circuit, how to read the schematic, which analysis the lab uses, and what the results mean. No electronics experience is needed.

Lessons open in a new tab, so this list stays open. Your progress is saved in this browser only. The course glossary (opens in a new tab) defines every key term.
